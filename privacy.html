Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Contentify is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, it uploads videos and reads channel and video statistics using the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

Tokens are stored privately by the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising, and no data about other users is collected.

Contentify's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
